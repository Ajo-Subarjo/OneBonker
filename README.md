# Whack-a-Mole 3D

A tiny 3D whack-a-mole game with a cartoon look, built with Three.js. The whole game fits in 3 KB.


## How to open the game
- go to the dist folder
- copy the uri
- and paste to the browser


## How to play

- Tap or click anywhere to start.
- Moles pop up from a 3x3 grid of holes. Tap a mole before it ducks back down to score a point.
- You have 30 seconds. Try to beat your best score.
- When time runs out, your final score is shown. Tap again to play another round.

## Features

- Toon-shaded 3D graphics with bold black outlines
- Moles with eyes, popping up at random
- Sound effects for popping up, hitting and missing
- Looping background music
- Score and countdown timer on screen
- All audio is generated in the browser, no sound files

## Requirements
A modern browser with WebGL and an internet connection (Three.js loads from a CDN). Sound turns on after your first tap.
